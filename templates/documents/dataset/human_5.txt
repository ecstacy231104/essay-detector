
I used to think debate was about winning arguments. Then I lost a final round arguing a position I actually believed in, against someone arguing the opposite who was, frankly, better prepared than me. It stung for weeks. But something about losing that particular round taught me more than any win had. I went back and read everything my opponent cited. Half of it I'd never even heard of. I changed my mind about the actual issue, not just the debate technique. That's not supposed to happen in competitive debate. Nobody warns you it might.
