
My dad's restaurant failed twice before it worked. I was too young to understand the first time. The second time I was twelve, old enough to see him counting coins from the register at night, old enough to hear the fights with my mom about money we didn't have. He didn't quit. He changed the menu, cut half the staff, worked the grill himself six days a week. I started helping after school, mostly folding napkins badly and getting yelled at for standing in the way. It's still open. Barely, some months. I don't know if that's a success story or just stubbornness. Maybe there's no difference.
