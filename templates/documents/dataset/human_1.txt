



I broke my wrist the summer before ninth grade, which sounds like a small thing until you realize I play violin. Six weeks in a cast meant six weeks of watching everyone else practice. My teacher told me to keep listening even if I couldn't play. I hated that advice. I sat in on lessons doing nothing with my hands, just listening, and somewhere around week four something shifted. I started hearing things I'd never noticed before, phrasing choices, where a player breathed, the tiny hesitation before a difficult passage. When the cast came off I was worse technically. But I played differently. Slower. More deliberate. My teacher noticed before I said anything.
