
My little sister has autism and doesn't speak much. People assume that means she doesn't communicate, which is wrong in a way that used to make me furious. She hums specific songs when she's anxious. She lines up her shoes by color when something at school upset her. I learned her language before I learned to explain it to other people, and honestly I'm still bad at explaining it. Last year a substitute teacher told her to stop humming in class. My sister cried for two hours that night. I wrote the school a very angry email. I'm not sorry.
