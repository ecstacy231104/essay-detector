
Our house flooded when I was fourteen. Not dramatically, just enough water to ruin the carpet and half our furniture. I remember my mom laughing, actually laughing, while we carried soggy boxes to the curb. I asked her what was funny. She said if she didn't laugh she'd start crying and wouldn't stop. I think about that a lot now, whenever something goes wrong that's out of my control. Some things you just have to decide how you're going to hold them. She taught me that without meaning to teach me anything.
