
I've never been good at sports. Not bad exactly, just unremarkable, the kid picked somewhere in the middle. So when my school announced a robotics club I signed up mostly out of relief that it wasn't gym class. Turns out I'm not naturally good at robotics either. Our first robot caught fire during a demonstration, which the teacher called a learning experience and I called deeply embarrassing. We rebuilt it three times. By regionals it worked, mostly. We came in ninth out of twelve teams. I've never been prouder of losing.
